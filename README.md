Step 1: Meet with Dr. Corey

Step 2: Create mentee profile on LFX Mentorship

Step 3: Check Mentorship Program Schedule and pick a term

Step 4: Complete the A Beginner’s Guide to Linux Kernel Development e-course

  Chapter 1. Course Introduction

  Chapter 2. Linux Kernel Development Process

  Chapter 3. Patches

Step 5: Upload the LKD certificate of completion

Step 6: Subscribe to linux-kernel-mentees list

Step 7: Join #kernel-mentees IRC Channel on OFTC.

Step 8: Upload resume, cover-letter

Step 9: Complete Skill evaluation task(s) assigned to you as task(s) in LFX Mentorship.

Step 10: Work on mentor assigned tasks to learn your project area.

Step 11: Make small contributions to the projects

Step 12: Report contributions

Step 13: Bugfix report contributions

Step 14: Complete all tasks assigned to you and submit them.
